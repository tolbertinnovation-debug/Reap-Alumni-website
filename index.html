Who we are

A lifelong bridge from training to transformation.

The REAP Alumni Association brings together graduates of REAP’s Vocational, Technical & Career Institute and related programs. We help alumni remain connected, strengthen their careers and businesses, mentor new graduates, and contribute to the communities that helped shape them.

Our mission

To connect, support, and mobilize REAP alumni for professional growth, principled leadership, entrepreneurship, mentorship, and community service.

Our vision

A united and active alumni community whose skills, character, and service create opportunity and lasting change in Liberia and beyond.
